CHAPTER 16 — MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & LINEAGE

AeroIndex Jevons Contribution Formula

Rigorous mathematical lineage defining exact basis-point attribution under geometric mean index theory
$$\text{Contribution}_{r,c} = W_r \cdot \left[ \left( \prod_{i \in \Omega_{r,c}} \frac{p_{i,t}}{p_{i,0}} \right)^{\frac{1}{N_{r,c}}} - 1 \right] \times 10{,}000 \text{ bps}$$
Where $W_r$ is the static corridor capacity weight, $\Omega_{r,c}$ is the verified set of matched quotes for route $r$ and carrier $c$, $p_{i,t}$ is the current price quote, $p_{i,0}$ is the baseline quote, and $10{,}000$ scales the geometric ratio to basis points.

Aviation Coverage & Observability Epistemological Standard

1. Coverage vs. Source Health

Source Health measures whether a provider API or scraper adapter is functioning. National Coverage measures what portion of the domestic aviation market AeroIndex can actually observe as a result. Coverage is a prerequisite for interpreting an index, but coverage percentage alone does not establish statistical representativeness. AeroIndex strictly enforces DGCA passenger traffic weights and an 80% elementary cell coverage guard before computing Jevons composite price changes.

3. Explicit Denominator Disclosure

AeroIndex never reports arbitrary percentage scores. Every metric exposes its exact numerator (observed valid units) and denominator (discovered domestic market universe) alongside timestamped collection audit logs.

AeroIndex / FareOS Mathematical & Governance Methodology

Official regulatory specification for high-frequency domestic airfare tracking in India, replacing manual CPI air travel sampling with transparent, un-biased index compilation.

1. The Elementary Jevons Index Formula

At the elementary cell level (Route $r \times$ Lead Bucket $b \times$ Day-of-Week $w$), the unweighted Jevons price relative avoids the upward substitution bias of Dutot and Carli formulations:

I_Jevons(t, 0) = [ ∏_{i=1}^n ( p_{i,t} / p_{i,0} ) ]^(1/n) = [ exp( (1/n) * ∑_{i=1}^n ln(p_{i,t}) ) / exp( (1/n) * ∑_{i=1}^n ln(p_{i,0}) ) ] × 100

2. Basket Weight Aggregation & Coverage Guard

Elementary cells are aggregated to the National Composite Index using DGCA passenger traffic route shares ($W_r$) and lead-time distribution weights ($w_b$). A strict minimum 80% coverage guard is required before official publication.

3. Dual Real-Time Architecture

The system strictly separates the continuous observation layer from scheduled collection. Observations continuously update the FLASH Redis engine for live distribution, while the 4 daily cycles (00:30, 06:30, 12:30, 18:30 IST) establish the frozen settlement index at 23:30 IST.
